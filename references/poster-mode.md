# Poster Mode

Use this mode for a single theme, dominant subject, and short display title.

## Preferred structures

Choose the structure that best fits the subject:

1. **Asymmetric landmark**: subject on the left or right third; a ribbon, road, vine, wind line, or shadow creates an S-curve; title occupies the opposing quiet field.
2. **Cropped still life**: multiple large specimens enter from the edges; one central negative-space pocket holds the title.
3. **Single monumental object**: one oversized subject occupies 45–65% of the frame, but its axis remains off-center; small secondary motifs balance the empty side.

Default aspect ratio is portrait 3:4 unless the user specifies otherwise.

## Prompt scaffold

Include only relevant fields:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept or precise-object-edit
Asset type: portrait art poster
Primary request: theme, subject, and intended meaning
Input images: assign style reference, layout reference, and edit target separately
Composition: off-center focal subject, visual path, negative-space title zone
Typography: exact quoted title, font character, position, scale
Style/medium: mineral fresco, weathered plaster, constructivist collage, distressed letterpress
Palette: brick red, terracotta, aged cream, muted blue-gray, olive, charcoal
Texture hierarchy: focal medium, background subtle, title zone quiet, lower field restrained
Invariants: elements that must not move or change
Avoid: random text, centered monumentality, fragmented bottom field, glossy/3D/vector drift
```

## Edit discipline

When revising an approved poster, name the exact region to change and repeat every invariant. For example: “modify only the bottom 20%; merge fragments into 3–5 larger fields; keep the subject, ribbon, birds, title, spelling, and upper texture unchanged.”

After generation, reject a version if the subject is centered against the user's direction, the title is misspelled, the quiet field becomes busy, or new symbolic content appears without authorization.
