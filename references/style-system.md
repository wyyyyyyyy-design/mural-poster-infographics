# Style System

## Visual DNA

The target look combines a weathered public mural, mineral-pigment fresco, abstract constructivist collage, and distressed letterpress printing. It must feel physically painted onto old lime plaster rather than simulated with a generic grunge filter.

### Palette

- Dominant: low-saturation brick red, terracotta, clay orange, ochre.
- Ground: aged cream, warm plaster white, sand, pale beige.
- Supporting: muted blue-gray, olive, gray-green, dark brown, charcoal.
- Avoid bright primary red, red-and-gold celebration palettes, fluorescent accents, and large areas of pure black.

### Surface and marks

Use irregular flat color fields, worn edges, mineral speckle, shallow plaster cracks, dry-brush drag, faded overprint, limited drips, and occasional scraped layers. Texture should vary by hierarchy:

- Focal subject: strongest color contrast and medium texture.
- Background: broad fields with subtle cracks.
- Typography zone: flatter and quieter for readability.
- Lower closure: 3–5 large fields rather than many small mosaic fragments.

Default to a balanced texture level. Heavy distress is appropriate only when explicitly requested.

### Composition

- Prefer asymmetric thirds, diagonal balance, cropped edges, S-curves, and controlled negative space.
- Use one primary visual path and no more than two secondary paths.
- Do not place a monument, tower, figure, or product directly at dead center by default.
- Let quiet wall fields counterbalance dense painted areas.

### Typography

- Hero Latin titles: condensed slab serif or heavy editorial serif, uppercase, charcoal or near-black.
- Character: distressed letterpress, slight ink loss, restrained misregistration; still readable at first glance.
- Chinese headings: clear bold sans or sturdy Song-style serif when appropriate; avoid decorative pseudo-calligraphy.
- Use exact quoted copy. Do not invent slogans, brands, dates, page numbers, or captions.

### Prohibited drift

Do not drift toward glossy food photography, cinematic photorealism, 3D plastic surfaces, neon cyberpunk, smooth vector art, cute illustration, generic guochao ornament, Dunhuang imitation, or revolutionary-propaganda motifs unless explicitly requested.
